# DOL Mythology Research Library

## Status
Phase 0 — Step 3: Mythology Research

## Purpose

This directory is the research layer for Dawn of Longlat.

It exists to document African mythological traditions accurately before DOL turns any material into fictional characters, locations, creatures, artifacts, abilities, quests, or story elements.

## Research Rule

> **Research first. Fiction second.**

DOL is a fictional game universe. Research notes must never be written as though different African traditions form one historical mythology.

## Source Quality

Prioritize:

1. Academic publications and university resources.
2. Museums, archives, libraries, and cultural institutions.
3. Primary historical/ethnographic sources where appropriate.
4. Reputable cultural organizations.
5. Carefully evaluated secondary sources.

Avoid using unsourced social-media posts, mythology-list websites, AI-generated summaries, or copied lists as the foundation of canon.

## Research Ethics

DOL should:

- distinguish living traditions from ancient historical records;
- recognize regional and community variation;
- avoid presenting sacred practices as generic fantasy mechanics without context;
- avoid inventing quotations or "traditional facts";
- preserve attribution in internal research;
- distinguish historical evidence from interpretation;
- distinguish research from DOL invention.

## Research Coverage

The long-term library should eventually cover diverse regions including:

- West Africa
- North Africa
- Central Africa
- East Africa
- Southern Africa
- African island cultures and traditions where relevant

This is a research roadmap, not a claim that these regions share one mythology.

## Initial Research Queue

The first research wave should deliberately provide geographic and cultural breadth.

### West Africa
- Yoruba traditions
- Akan traditions
- Fon/Ewe traditions
- Mande-related traditions

### North Africa
- Ancient Egyptian religious traditions
- Amazigh/Berber traditional belief systems

### Central Africa
- Kongo traditions
- selected Central African traditions requiring further research

### East Africa
- Maasai traditions
- Oromo traditions
- selected Ethiopian/Eritrean traditions requiring further research

### Southern Africa
- Zulu traditions
- Xhosa traditions
- San traditions

### Important Note

These are starting research categories, not a final list and not interchangeable groups.

Each research record must be treated on its own terms.

## Mythology-to-Gameplay Pipeline

Research should eventually flow through:

**Real tradition**
→ **Research record**
→ **DOL interpretation**
→ **Original fictional design**
→ **Gameplay mechanic**
→ **Character / creature / artifact / location**

The final game should feel coherent while retaining traceable research behind its inspirations.

## First Deliverable

Create the first fully researched tradition record before designing any major mythological character.

Suggested starting research:

**Akan traditions**, because Longlat's initial setting is in the Heartlands and the first region should have a carefully researched cultural foundation.

This is only a starting point; it does not establish Akan tradition as the "main" mythology of DOL.
