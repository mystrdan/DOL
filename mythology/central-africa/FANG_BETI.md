# Fang and Beti traditions

> **Research status: Preliminary — not DOL canon.**

This is a dedicated research track. It must be treated as a distinct community/tradition unless reliable scholarship establishes a narrower or broader relationship.

## Research Areas
- People/community names, language, geography, history, and internal subgroups
- Cosmology and origin narratives
- Supreme-being concepts, spirits, ancestors, heroes, tricksters, and other supernatural beings where documented
- Sacred landscapes, animals, plants, objects, symbols, architecture, and material culture
- Oral literature, epics, folktales, praise, music, dance, performance, and historical traditions
- Divination, healing, initiation, festivals, rites, specialists, and institutions where appropriate
- Migration, trade, state formation, Christianity/Islam, colonial history, diaspora, and contemporary practice

## Sources
Prioritize community/cultural institutions, academic scholarship, museums/archives/libraries, archaeology, and primary or early sources read critically. Secondary summaries are discovery aids, not final evidence.

## Classification
**DOCUMENTED** / **INTERPRETATION** / **DOL ORIGINAL** / **UNVERIFIED**.

## Adaptation Gate
Research does not automatically become gameplay. Sacred figures, rites, symbols, objects, stories, or living practices require context and a documented adaptation decision before appearing in DOL.

## Open Questions
- Which subgroups require separate records?
- Which sources represent community voices?
- Which variations matter across regions or periods?
- What belongs in lore rather than combat/gameplay?
- What original DOL material can be inspired without claiming historical authenticity?

**Research first. Fiction second. Gameplay third.**
