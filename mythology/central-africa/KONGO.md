# Kongo Traditions — Preliminary Research Record

> **Status: Research draft — not DOL canon.**

## 1. Identity & Scope

Kongo traditions are associated with Kongo-speaking peoples and historical Kongo political societies in parts of present-day Angola, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, and Republic of the Congo.

Research must distinguish historical Kongo religion, regional variation, later Christian influence, and neighboring Central African traditions.

## 2. Research Areas

Investigate:
- Nzambi/Nzambi Mpungu and creator concepts
- ancestors and the relationship between living and dead
- spiritual forces and intermediary beings
- sacred landscapes and water
- cosmological ideas concerning life/death and the visible/invisible worlds
- Kongo political and ritual institutions
- material culture and symbolic systems
- Kongo Christianity and religious change after European contact

## 3. Visual & Material Research

The **Kongo cosmogram/Yowa-related symbolic tradition** is a major research direction, but its meanings and historical interpretations must be sourced carefully.

Do not turn sacred diagrams into generic skill trees or loot symbols without context.

## 4. Figures & Beings

Potential research subjects:
- Nzambi/Nzambi Mpungu
- Simbi/simbi-related spiritual beings
- Bunzi and regional traditions
- ancestors and specialist ritual roles

No subject is approved as a DOL character or enemy.

## 5. Evidence

Prioritize:
- Kongo historical sources
- museum collections
- archaeology
- academic anthropology/religious studies
- contemporary Kongo scholarship and cultural voices
