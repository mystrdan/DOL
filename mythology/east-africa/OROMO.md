# Oromo / Waaqeffanna — Preliminary Research Record

> **Status: Research draft — not DOL canon.**

## 1. Identity & Scope

Oromo peoples are distributed primarily across Ethiopia and Kenya, with substantial regional and historical diversity.

**Waaqeffanna** refers to Oromo indigenous religious traditions. It should be studied as a living tradition rather than as a catalogue of ancient gods.

## 2. Research Areas

Investigate:
- Waaqa and creator/supreme-being concepts
- sacred natural environments
- ayyaana and spiritual categories
- ancestors and community
- moral order and social responsibility
- ritual specialists and institutions
- oral traditions
- historical interaction with Christianity and Islam
- regional variation across Oromo communities

## 3. Representation Rules

Do not turn Waaqa into a conventional fantasy “boss” or treat Waaqeffanna as a generic elemental magic system.

Research living Oromo perspectives alongside historical scholarship.
