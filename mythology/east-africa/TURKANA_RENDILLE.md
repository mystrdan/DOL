# Turkana and Rendille Traditions

> **Research status: Preliminary — not DOL canon.**

Separate research tracks for Turkana and Rendille communities of northern Kenya and neighboring regions.

## Research areas
- Community identities and languages
- Pastoral ecology and mobility
- Cosmology and creator concepts
- Spirits, ancestors and sacred landscapes
- Rain, drought, livestock and environmental relationships
- Oral poetry, genealogy and historical memory
- Ritual specialists and customary institutions
- Christianity/Islam and indigenous practice
- Contemporary perspectives

## DOL adaptation gate
Avoid reducing communities to “warrior,” “cattle,” or “desert” stereotypes. No living ritual or sacred object becomes game content without specific research.

**Classification:** DOCUMENTED / INTERPRETATION / DOL ORIGINAL / UNVERIFIED
