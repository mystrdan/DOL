# Maasai Traditions — Preliminary Research Record

> **Status: Research draft — not DOL canon.**

## 1. Identity & Scope

Maasai communities are primarily associated with Kenya and Tanzania. Their cultural and religious traditions should be studied through community-specific and scholarly sources.

## 2. Research Areas

Investigate:
- Enkai/Engai and supreme-being concepts
- relationships among people, cattle, land, rain, and environment
- ritual life and age-set institutions
- prophets and religious specialists
- oral traditions
- sacred landscapes
- regional variation
- historical and contemporary changes

## 3. Representation Rules

Do not reduce Maasai culture to:
- warriors
- cattle
- spears
- “tribal” aesthetics

The game should represent social systems, environment, philosophy, material culture, and history with equal seriousness.
