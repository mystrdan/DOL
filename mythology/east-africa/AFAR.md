# Afar Traditions

> **Research status: Preliminary — not DOL canon.**

## Research areas
- Afar identity, language and regional history
- Danakil/Afar ecology and mobility
- Oral poetry and historical memory
- Islam and local religious practice
- Spirits, sacred places and protective traditions
- Social organization and customary institutions
- Material culture and contemporary life

## Evidence standard
Use community sources, African scholarship, historical/anthropological research and archives. Record regional variation explicitly.

## DOL adaptation gate
Do not equate desert ecology with supernatural identity. Sacred practices remain contextual unless adaptation is justified by research.

**Classification:** DOCUMENTED / INTERPRETATION / DOL ORIGINAL / UNVERIFIED
