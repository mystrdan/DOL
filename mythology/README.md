# DOL Mythology Bible

This directory contains research for the mythological and cultural foundations that may inform Dawn of Longlat.

## Important rule

**Africa does not have one mythology.**

DOL therefore treats traditions as distinct bodies of knowledge. Research is organized by tradition rather than by a generic category such as African gods.

## Research template

For each tradition, investigate:

- Geographic and cultural context
- Cosmology
- Creator or supreme concepts
- Divine beings
- Spirits
- Ancestors
- Heroes and legendary figures
- Supernatural creatures
- Sacred objects
- Weapons and tools
- Symbols
- Sacred places
- Important stories
- Concepts and values
- Variations within the tradition
- Primary and academic sources
- Documented material
- Interpretation
- Potential DOL adaptation
- Material that should remain contextualized or untouched

## Adaptation rule

No real figure becomes a game mechanic merely because the mechanic sounds cool.

First understand the tradition. Then decide whether adaptation is appropriate. Then design the mechanic.

**Research → Context → Adaptation → Gameplay**
