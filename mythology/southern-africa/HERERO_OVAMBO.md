# Herero and Ovambo Traditions

> **Research status: Preliminary — not DOL canon.**

Separate research tracks for Herero-related communities and Ovambo communities of Namibia/Angola, recognizing internal diversity.

## Research areas
- Community names, languages and historical geography
- Pastoral/agricultural ecology
- Ancestors and sacred landscapes
- Creator/supreme concepts
- Oral history and praise
- Ritual specialists and healing
- Colonial and missionary transformations
- Contemporary religious/cultural life
- Material culture

## Evidence standard
Use Namibian/Angolan scholarship, community institutions, archives, museums and peer-reviewed research.

## DOL adaptation gate
Historical trauma and living identity must not be reduced to fantasy aesthetics. Sacred or ceremonial material requires contextual research.

**Classification:** DOCUMENTED / INTERPRETATION / DOL ORIGINAL / UNVERIFIED
