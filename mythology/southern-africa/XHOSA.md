# Xhosa Traditions — Preliminary Research Record

> **Status: Research draft — not DOL canon.**

## 1. Identity & Scope

Xhosa-speaking peoples and communities have diverse histories and traditions in southern Africa. Research should distinguish Xhosa traditions from broader Nguni categories.

## 2. Research Areas

Investigate:
- creator and cosmological concepts
- ancestors and communication with the living
- divination and healing
- sacred places and natural phenomena
- oral literature, praise, music, and storytelling
- historical community structures
- regional variation
- interactions with Christianity and colonialism

## 3. Representation Rules

Avoid reducing Xhosa culture to language, clothing, beadwork, or famous historical figures.

Sensitive ritual and healing traditions require careful contextual research before any gameplay adaptation.
