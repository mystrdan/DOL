# Zulu Traditions — Preliminary Research Record

> **Status: Research draft — not DOL canon.**

## 1. Identity & Scope

Zulu religious and oral traditions belong to a specific historical and cultural context in southern Africa. They should not be treated as interchangeable with all Nguni, Bantu-speaking, or southern African traditions.

## 2. Research Areas

Investigate:
- creator concepts and traditions associated with uNkulunkulu
- ancestors/amadlozi
- diviners and healing traditions
- sacred landscapes
- oral narratives and praise poetry
- historical Zulu society and political change
- relationships between religion, community, health, and morality
- regional and historical variation

## 3. Representation Rules

Do not reduce Zulu culture to Shaka, warfare, shields, or “warrior mythology.”

Historical Zulu military culture may inform worldbuilding where appropriate, but religious traditions require separate research.
