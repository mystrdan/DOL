# San Traditions — Preliminary Research Record

> **Status: Research draft — not DOL canon.**
>
> “San” is a broad umbrella label covering multiple communities and languages. Research must avoid treating all San peoples as one culture.

## 1. Research Areas

Investigate community-specific traditions across southern Africa, including:
- !Kung and related traditions where sources support them
- Ju|'hoansi and other specific communities
- regional cosmologies
- trance/healing traditions
- animal relationships and symbolism
- rock art and its interpretation
- trickster/creator narratives
- landscape, water, and sacred geography
- oral performance and storytelling

## 2. Critical Rule

Rock art must not automatically be interpreted as a literal record of one universal San mythology. Archaeological interpretation is often contested and must be separated from living community traditions.

## 3. Representation

Avoid the stereotype of the San as a single “primitive hunter” culture.

Where gameplay inspiration is considered, focus on specific documented communities, environment, knowledge systems, social organization, and storytelling rather than generic survival stereotypes.
