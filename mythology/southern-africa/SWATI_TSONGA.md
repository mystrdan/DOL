# Swati and Tsonga Traditions

> **Research status: Preliminary — not DOL canon.**

Separate research tracks for Swati/Eswatini communities and Tsonga communities across southern Africa.

## Research areas
- Community and language identity
- Cosmology and creator concepts
- Ancestors and spirits
- Divination and healing
- Sacred landscapes
- Oral poetry, performance and historical memory
- Kingship/social institutions
- Christianity and indigenous practice
- Regional variation and contemporary life

## Evidence standard
Prioritize community sources, southern African scholarship, museums/archives and peer-reviewed research.

## DOL adaptation gate
Do not turn royal, ancestral, healing or ritual traditions into generic fantasy classes or powers.

**Classification:** DOCUMENTED / INTERPRETATION / DOL ORIGINAL / UNVERIFIED
