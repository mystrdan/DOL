# Game 1 Religious Coexistence Research

## Purpose

Longlat should depict religion as part of ordinary social life rather than as three competing fantasy factions.

## Evidence anchors

University of Ghana research on Christian engagement with Muslims in Ghana documents constructive interfaith engagement and peaceful coexistence as a significant part of the Ghanaian context. citeturn1search0

MIASA research on religious diversity in urban West Africa examines how migration, mobility, religious infrastructure, authority, and everyday social milieus shape coexistence. Its Ghana-related work includes Christians, Muslims, and practitioners of African Religious Traditions in Accra. citeturn1search2turn1search29

University of Ghana research on contemporary Akwamu society includes family heads, chiefs, queen mothers, Christians, and Muslims in its study of traditional ethical practices, showing that religious identity can overlap with wider community institutions rather than existing as isolated game factions. citeturn1search7

Research on Konkomba society describes traditional religious practices alongside Christianity and Islam, with religion connected to social structures, political systems, worldview, moral values, and community cohesion. citeturn1search25

## DOL implications

- A Longlat settlement may contain different religious communities without requiring them to become opposing factions.
- A church, mosque, and traditional/community sacred place should each have an ordinary social function before the Dawn event affects them.
- NPCs can disagree about the meaning of an anomaly without becoming representatives of an entire religion.
- Religious spaces should not be used as generic quest hubs or supernatural power stations.
- Traditional religion must not be presented as “the magic religion” while Christianity and Islam are treated as non-magical background.
- Christianity and Islam should not be reduced to imported stereotypes; their local histories and lived forms matter.
- Specific sacred practices, symbols, clergy, shrines, rituals, and language require separate research and adaptation records before final implementation.

## Game 1 use

For the first prototype, keep religious representation mostly environmental and social:

- ordinary gathering/worship spaces;
- NPC relationships;
- community routines;
- different interpretations of the same disturbance;
- no playable religious power system.

The supernatural layer remains DOL-original.

## Research gate

Before adding a named real-world religious figure, sacred object, ritual, prayer, scripture reference, shrine, church tradition, mosque tradition, or traditional religious practice:

**specific community/context → source → variation → classification → adaptation decision → review**

## Status

Research direction only. No real-world religious material is approved for direct copying into final DOL assets.
