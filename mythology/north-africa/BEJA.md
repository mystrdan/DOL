# Beja Traditions

> **Research status: Preliminary — not DOL canon.**

Research track for Beja communities of northeastern Africa, with attention to internal diversity and historical change.

## Research areas
- Beja community names, languages and regional groups
- Desert/coastal ecology and mobility
- Oral traditions and historical memory
- Cosmology, spirits and ancestors
- Sacred landscapes and protective traditions
- Islam and older/local practices
- Material culture and poetry
- Contemporary community perspectives

## Evidence standard
Prioritize African scholarship, community/cultural sources, archaeology, museums/archives and peer-reviewed research.

## DOL adaptation gate
Living religious practice and sacred symbols require contextual treatment and must not be converted directly into generic fantasy mechanics.

**Classification:** DOCUMENTED / INTERPRETATION / DOL ORIGINAL / UNVERIFIED
