# Ga-Dangme Traditions

> **Research status: Preliminary — not DOL canon.**

A dedicated track for Ga and Dangme communities of coastal southeastern Ghana and nearby areas. Ga-Dangme should not be treated as a generic “Ghanaian mythology.”

## Research areas
- Ga and Dangme identities, languages and regional variation
- Coastal ecology, settlement and historical trade
- Creator/supreme-being concepts
- Divinities, spirits and ancestors
- Sacred landscapes, shrines and ritual specialists
- Festivals, oral traditions and historical memory
- Material culture and visual symbolism
- Christianity, Islam and indigenous practice
- Contemporary community perspectives

## Evidence standard
Use community/cultural institutions, Ghanaian and African scholarship, archives, museums and peer-reviewed research. Older colonial ethnography must be contextualized.

## DOL adaptation gate
Do not turn sacred beings, shrines, festivals, ritual objects or living practices into generic fantasy mechanics without specific research and contextual review.

**Classification:** DOCUMENTED / INTERPRETATION / DOL ORIGINAL / UNVERIFIED
