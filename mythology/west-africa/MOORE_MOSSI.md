# Mossi/Moore traditions

> **Research status: Preliminary — not DOL canon.**

This is a dedicated research track, not a claim that the named communities share one uniform mythology. Internal variation, language, locality, history, and contemporary practice must be documented before adaptation.

## Research Areas
- Community names, subgroup names, languages, geography, migration, and historical context
- Cosmology, origin narratives, divine/supreme-being concepts, spirits, ancestors, heroes and legendary figures where documented
- Sacred landscapes, animals, plants, objects, symbols, architecture and material culture
- Oral literature, epics, folktales, praise traditions, music, dance and performance
- Divination, healing, initiation, festivals, rites and ritual specialists where appropriate
- Political institutions, social organization, trade and environmental relationships where they illuminate religious life
- Christian, Islamic, colonial, diaspora and modern influences; continuity and change

## Evidence
Prioritize community/cultural institutions, African scholarship, peer-reviewed research, museums/archives/libraries, archaeology, and primary or early sources read critically. Colonial terminology must be treated as historical evidence, not automatically as the community's own category.

## Classification
- **DOCUMENTED** — supported by reliable evidence
- **INTERPRETATION** — scholarly/comparative interpretation
- **DOL ORIGINAL** — invented for Dawn of Longlat
- **UNVERIFIED** — requires further verification

## Adaptation Gate
No documented sacred figure, ritual, symbol, object, or living practice automatically becomes a DOL enemy, ability, weapon, costume, collectible, or quest. Every adaptation requires context, source attribution, transformation notes, gameplay/story purpose, and representation review.

## Coverage Questions
- Which named subgroups require separate records?
- Which alternate names and spellings must be indexed?
- Which traditions are distinct despite shared language-family labels?
- Which sources include community voices?
- Which elements should remain lore/research rather than gameplay?

**Research first. Fiction second. Gameplay third.**
