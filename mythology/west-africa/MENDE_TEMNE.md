# Mende and Temne Traditions

> **Research status: Preliminary — not DOL canon.**

Separate research tracks for Mende and Temne communities of Sierra Leone and neighboring areas. Similar geography does not mean identical traditions.

## Research areas
- Community and language identity
- Political and social institutions
- Cosmology and creator concepts
- Ancestors and spirits
- Initiation and secret societies — contextual research only
- Oral literature, masks and performance
- Healing, divination and ritual specialists
- Sacred landscapes and material culture
- Colonial and postcolonial transformations
- Contemporary religious life

## Evidence standard
Prioritize African scholarship, community/cultural institutions, museums, archives and peer-reviewed ethnography. Treat colonial descriptions critically.

## DOL adaptation gate
Initiation societies, masks, ritual specialists and sacred objects require special sensitivity. They are not automatically classes, factions, armor sets or abilities.

**Classification:** DOCUMENTED / INTERPRETATION / DOL ORIGINAL / UNVERIFIED
