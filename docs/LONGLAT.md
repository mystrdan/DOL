# Longlat City Foundation

## Status
Phase 0 — Step 2: World Foundation

## 1. Identity

Longlat is a prosperous ancient crossroads civilization built around trade, water, knowledge, and an older mystery beneath the city.

It is neither the capital of the entire continent nor a stand-in for any historical African city.

Its importance comes from its position and history.

## 2. Why Longlat Exists Here

Longlat grew beside a major river and along an ancient overland route.

Three systems meet here:

- river transport;
- overland trade;
- access toward the highlands and forest.

This made Longlat a natural meeting point for different peoples.

## 3. The Three Layers of Longlat

### The Living City

Markets, homes, workshops, farms, public spaces, places of learning, defensive structures, river docks, and places of worship.

### The Old City

Structures beneath and around the modern civilization.

Some are abandoned.

Some have been incorporated into newer buildings.

Some entrances are deliberately sealed.

### The Deep Place

A hidden location beneath Longlat connected to the ancient boundary between worlds.

Its true purpose is unknown at the beginning of the game.

This location is central to the mystery of the Dawn.

## 4. City Districts — Working Layout

### The River Quarter

The city's economic gateway.

Contains:

- docks;
- warehouses;
- boatyards;
- fish markets;
- traders;
- inns;
- river gates.

Gameplay role: commerce, NPC encounters, information, early quests.

### The Market Quarter

The social heart of Longlat.

Contains:

- food vendors;
- artisans;
- merchants;
- performers;
- travelers;
- public gathering spaces.

Gameplay role: equipment, rumors, side quests, character interactions.

### The High Quarter

Elevated part of the city.

Contains:

- major civic buildings;
- wealthy residences;
- archives;
- important institutions;
- defensive positions.

Gameplay role: politics, lore, major story conversations.

### The Old Quarter

The oldest visible part of the city.

Contains:

- ancient walls;
- abandoned structures;
- old carvings;
- sealed doors;
- forgotten paths.

Gameplay role: exploration and mystery.

### The Outer Fields

Agricultural territory beyond the dense city.

Contains:

- farms;
- small settlements;
- grazing areas;
- roads;
- watch posts.

Gameplay role: tutorial exploration and early combat.

## 5. The Old Road

The ancient road is older than modern Longlat.

Its original builders are unknown.

The road passes through the region and appears to continue toward places that no longer exist.

After the Dawn, sections of the road can lead to locations that should be physically unreachable.

This is the first major indication that the geography itself has changed.

## 6. The River

Working name: **The Great River**

The final name remains open.

The river should be a major geographic feature rather than a magical object.

It supports ordinary life first.

After the Dawn, unusual events begin occurring around certain sections.

## 7. Longlat Before the Dawn

Longlat was prosperous but not perfect.

It had:

- competing interests;
- social divisions;
- political tensions;
- trade disputes;
- old rivalries;
- religious and philosophical disagreements.

This keeps the civilization believable.

The Dawn does not create all conflict. It amplifies existing tensions.

## 8. The Hidden Mystery

The founders of Longlat discovered something beneath the region.

Rather than fully understanding it, they built structures around it.

Over generations, knowledge about the original purpose was fragmented.

Different groups preserved different pieces of the truth.

By the beginning of the game, almost nobody understands the complete picture.

## 9. The Dawn Begins

The first visible signs occur around Longlat.

Initial symptoms:

- strange changes in the sky;
- animals behaving abnormally;
- old structures becoming accessible;
- people reporting impossible sounds or figures;
- sections of the Old Road appearing altered;
- the first Wound opening.

The player does not immediately receive an explanation.

## 10. First Playable Sequence

The game should begin with ordinary life.

The player learns movement and interaction inside or near Longlat before the supernatural becomes unavoidable.

Suggested sequence:

1. Wake / begin daily activity.
2. Explore a small section of Longlat.
3. Meet important early characters.
4. Establish ordinary life.
5. Receive a simple objective outside the central district.
6. Encounter the first abnormal event.
7. Return toward Longlat.
8. Discover that something has changed.
9. First combat encounter.
10. Enter the first Wound.
11. End the opening sequence with the player seeing evidence that the event is much older than it appears.

## 11. Design Principle

**Start human. Become legendary.**

The player should first experience Longlat as a living civilization.

Only then should the supernatural scale of DOL begin to unfold.

## 12. Canon Status

Canon:
- Longlat is an ancient prosperous crossroads.
- It developed around a river and an ancient road.
- It contains living, old, and hidden layers.
- The city has political and social complexity.
- The Deep Place exists beneath Longlat.
- The Dawn's first visible effects occur around Longlat.

Open:
- final river name;
- city architecture;
- government structure;
- language;
- population;
- exact founding date;
- identity of the founders;
- exact nature of the Deep Place;
- protagonist;
- first enemy faction.
