# DOL First Veil Design

## Status
Game 1 — Supernatural systems prototype

## Purpose

The first Veil is the moment DOL changes from an unusual human world into a supernatural game world.

It must be memorable without requiring a large effects budget.

## What the Veil Is

A **Veil** is a fictional DOL boundary between layers of reality.

The concept is original to DOL.

It should not be presented as a universal term from African religions.

## Location

The first Veil sits beyond the river and sacred landscape, along an older route.

The player should reach it naturally rather than through a giant fantasy landmark visible from the settlement.

## Three-State Presentation

### State 1 — Dormant

Before the player approaches:
- ordinary landscape;
- normal ambient sound;
- ordinary wildlife;
- subtle damaged marker or environmental clue.

### State 2 — Disturbed

Near the boundary:
- sound becomes spatially strange;
- movement in vegetation becomes difficult to interpret;
- distant objects appear slightly displaced;
- wildlife avoids a specific area.

Use one strong effect rather than many simultaneous effects.

### State 3 — Torn

During the encounter:
- the boundary becomes visibly unstable;
- the environment responds to the creature;
- the player understands that something has crossed.

## First Mechanical Rule

**Near a damaged Veil, the world can briefly violate normal expectations.**

Prototype one effect:

### Spatial Echo

A nearby object briefly appears in the wrong position before snapping back.

Gameplay use:
- teaches that the Veil changes perception/space;
- creates tension;
- provides a visual cue before enemy activity.

Do not make the player solve a puzzle yet.

## Audio

Audio should carry much of the supernatural identity.

Possible design:
- normal environmental bed;
- subtle directional distortion near the Veil;
- brief silence before a major event;
- creature sound that does not map cleanly to a recognizable animal.

Avoid relying on stereotyped “tribal” drums to announce supernatural danger.

## Visual Language

The Veil should have:
- a recognizable silhouette or boundary;
- one repeatable visual behavior;
- restrained effects;
- readable gameplay space.

Avoid:
- generic purple portal;
- giant glowing magic circle;
- excessive particles;
- fantasy smoke covering the whole screen.

## Narrative Function

The Veil answers one question:

**The Dawn is physically changing the world.**

It creates another:

**What else can cross?**

## Research Boundary

The Veil itself is DOL-original.

If later research inspires a culturally specific sacred boundary, shrine, cosmological threshold, or supernatural location, that material must be documented separately before adaptation.

Do not retroactively claim that the DOL Veil is a representation of a real-world belief.

## Prototype Success

The player should be able to describe the Veil after leaving it:

> “Something was wrong with reality there.”

That is enough for Version 1.
