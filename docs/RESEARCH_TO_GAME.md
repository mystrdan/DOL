# Research → Game Pipeline

## Purpose

Keep research broad while preventing research from becoming the bottleneck for Game 1.

## Three Research Levels

### Level 1 — Continental Awareness

Maintain broad coverage of African communities, traditions, regions, historical contexts, and living practices.

Goal: prevent DOL from confusing a small number of traditions with Africa as a whole.

### Level 2 — Game 1 Research

Deep research only for traditions, communities, places, histories, or religious/cultural elements that directly influence Game 1.

Each significant element requires:
- source;
- context;
- variation;
- classification;
- adaptation decision.

### Level 3 — Gameplay Adaptation

Only after Levels 1 and 2 can an element become:
- character inspiration;
- enemy;
- location;
- ability;
- quest;
- item;
- visual motif;
- dialogue/lore.

## Stop Conditions

Do not keep researching an element indefinitely once:
- the relevant context is understood;
- variations affecting the game are recorded;
- adaptation risks are identified;
- the game role is clear.

Then build and test.

## Core Principle

Research exists to make the game better and more responsible.

**If research is not improving a decision, stop researching and prototype.**
