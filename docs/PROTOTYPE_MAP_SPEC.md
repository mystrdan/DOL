# DOL Prototype Map Specification

## Goal

Define the smallest physical map that can carry the first 10–15 minutes of DOL.

## Topology

**Longlat Gate → Settlement Edge → Common Yard → Old Road → River Crossing → Sacred/Community Landscape → Damaged Veil → Combat Clearing → Return Route**

## Zone 01 — Longlat Gate

Purpose:
- establish location;
- show movement;
- establish scale.

Elements:
- road;
- settlement silhouette;
- pedestrians;
- carts or practical transport where appropriate;
- cultivated/work areas.

No combat.

## Zone 02 — Settlement Edge

Purpose:
- prove this is a lived place.

Elements:
- homes;
- cooking/work activity;
- people;
- small goods;
- animals where research supports them;
- maintenance/repair;
- paths between spaces.

Player can inspect and talk.

## Zone 03 — Common Yard

Purpose:
- first social interaction.

Contains:
- one progression NPC;
- optional NPCs;
- ordinary activity;
- first subtle Dawn anomaly.

The environment should remain useful even if all supernatural content is removed.

## Zone 04 — Old Road

Purpose:
- transition from ordinary to uncertain.

Elements:
- worn route;
- old marker;
- recent footprints/tracks;
- abandoned or displaced object;
- environmental clue.

Optional side pocket:
- one small observation reward;
- no combat required.

## Zone 05 — River Crossing

Purpose:
- change rhythm and establish geography.

Options:
- bridge;
- ford;
- ferry.

Pick one for the prototype based on final environmental research.

The river should be a spatial landmark, not merely a loading-screen boundary.

## Zone 06 — Sacred / Community Landscape

Purpose:
- show that religion/community exists as part of ordinary life.

Possible prototype representation:
- church;
- mosque;
- traditional sacred place;
- community gathering area.

The final choice must follow local research and fictionalization.

No automatic combat.
No generic “juju” treatment.
No sacred object as loot.

## Zone 07 — Damaged Veil

Purpose:
- first unmistakable supernatural location.

Design:
- recognizable landmark;
- minimal effects;
- spatial distortion;
- sound change;
- one repeatable Spatial Echo behavior.

## Zone 08 — Combat Clearing

Purpose:
- readable first fight.

Requirements:
- enough room to dodge;
- no confusing clutter;
- Veil visible from arena;
- one or two simple obstacles;
- clear escape/return route.

## Zone 09 — Return Route

Purpose:
- give the player a short decompression moment;
- deliver closing reveal;
- point back toward Longlat.

Do not add a second combat encounter.

## Navigation Rules

- Every major transition gets a landmark.
- Avoid identical paths.
- Avoid invisible walls where possible.
- Keep critical route visually obvious.
- Optional exploration must return naturally to the main route.
- Player should rarely need a minimap.

## Prototype Scale

The map should be small enough to build quickly and large enough to create:
- one social space;
- one travel space;
- one environmental landmark;
- one supernatural space;
- one combat space.

Do not build empty terrain simply to make the map feel large.
