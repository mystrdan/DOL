# DOL — First 15 Minutes Scene Script

## Status
Game 1 — Prototype script

## Purpose

Turn the existing first-15-minute design into something a developer and writer can prototype directly.

The scene should teach the player the world before teaching them the mythology.

## Scene 01 — Longlat Marches Arrival
**Target:** 0:00–2:00

### Player
- gains movement;
- sees settlement activity;
- can inspect one environmental object;
- can approach the first NPC.

### World
No combat.

Ambient activity:
- walking;
- work;
- conversation;
- food preparation;
- animals;
- distant Longlat traffic.

### Story signal
One ordinary thing is slightly wrong.

Examples:
- an animal refuses a familiar route;
- a traveler watches the old road instead of the market;
- a marker has been disturbed.

Do not explain it immediately.

## Scene 02 — The Question
**Target:** 2:00–5:00

The player encounters a person who has a practical reason to mention the old road.

The NPC should not say “the supernatural is coming.”

Instead:
- something happened;
- normal travel changed;
- someone has not returned;
- the river/road behaves differently.

### Player objective

**Find out what happened near the old road.**

The objective should be understandable without a lore lecture.

## Scene 03 — Old Road
**Target:** 5:00–8:00

The player leaves the settlement.

Optional observations:
- footprints;
- damaged marker;
- abandoned goods;
- animal tracks;
- alternate path.

### Design

At least one optional route should exist.

Reward exploration with information, not a powerful item.

## Scene 04 — River Crossing
**Target:** 8:00–10:00

The player reaches the river.

The environment becomes quieter.

One undeniable anomaly occurs.

Recommended prototype:
**Spatial Echo** — an object briefly appears somewhere it cannot physically be.

The player should be able to continue without solving anything.

## Scene 05 — Sacred Landscape
**Target:** 10:00–11:00

The player passes a community sacred/gathering location.

This is intentionally calm.

Possible inhabitants:
- worshipper;
- caretaker;
- traveler;
- community member.

The space demonstrates ordinary religious/social life.

No forced supernatural interpretation.

## Scene 06 — First Veil
**Target:** 11:00–13:00

Environmental audio shifts.

The player sees the damaged Veil.

The player approaches.

The Veil-Torn emerges.

No boss health bar.

## Scene 07 — First Combat
**Target:** 13:00–15:00

Teach:
1. move;
2. dodge;
3. attack;
4. recover;
5. recognize Veil Pulse.

The encounter ends with:
- defeat; or
- escape.

## Scene 08 — Closing Reveal

Immediately after the encounter:

A distant disturbance appears somewhere beyond the player's current route.

No explanation.

No mythology lecture.

Final emotional beat:

**The player realizes this was not an isolated accident.**

## Dialogue Philosophy

Dialogue should be short enough that the player can keep moving.

Prefer:
- “I saw something.”
- “The road wasn't like this yesterday.”
- “Don't go that way.”
- “Why?”
- “I don't know.”

over paragraphs explaining cosmology.

## Prototype Requirement

Every scene must be skippable or interruptible without breaking the game state.

## Success Test

After 15 minutes, a new player should know:

- where they are;
- what changed;
- what they are investigating;
- how to fight;
- that the world is larger than what they have seen.

They do **not** need to know the entire mythology.
