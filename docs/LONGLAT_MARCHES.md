# Longlat Marches

## Status
Game 1 — First playable region foundation

The Longlat Marches are the compact region immediately outside Longlat used for the first vertical slice.

## Purpose

The Marches introduce the player to:
- ordinary life around Longlat;
- the road and river systems that connect communities;
- religious and cultural diversity;
- the first visible effects of the Dawn;
- the first Veil;
- the first supernatural combat encounter.

The region is deliberately small.

## Layout

**Longlat Gate → Settlement Edge → Old Road → River Crossing → Sacred Landscape → Damaged Veil**

A short alternate path branches from the Old Road and rewards exploration.

## 1. Settlement Edge

A small fictional community outside Longlat's main walls.

The player can see:
- homes;
- food preparation;
- traders;
- children and adults;
- a place of worship or gathering;
- farmers/travelers moving between settlement and city.

The settlement exists before the supernatural event becomes the player's problem.

## 2. Old Road

An older trade and travel route leading toward Longlat.

Environmental storytelling:
- carts and footprints;
- roadside shade;
- small trading activity;
- damaged marker stones;
- evidence that people have recently stopped using part of the route.

## 3. River Crossing

A bridge, ford, or ferry crossing.

The river establishes:
- geography;
- travel;
- agriculture;
- visual scale;
- a natural boundary before the supernatural boundary is introduced.

It can also provide the first optional exploration pocket.

## 4. Sacred Landscape

A quiet location connected to the local fictional community.

This may include a church, mosque, traditional sacred place, or another community space depending on the final settlement design.

These locations are primarily social/worldbuilding spaces.

Do not automatically turn them into supernatural dungeons.

## 5. Damaged Veil

The first major supernatural location.

Before reaching it:
- wildlife behaves strangely;
- sound becomes subtly abnormal;
- a normal path feels increasingly empty;
- the environment shows signs of disturbance.

At the Veil:
- reality visibly behaves differently;
- the first Veil-Torn appears;
- the player learns that the Dawn is physical, not merely a rumor.

## Player Route

The critical route should take approximately 10–15 minutes for a first-time player.

Optional exploration can extend the session.

## Design Rule

The Marches should feel like a place people live in—not a combat corridor decorated with African-looking assets.
