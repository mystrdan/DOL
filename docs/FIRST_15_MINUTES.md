# DOL — First 15 Minutes

## Goal

The opening must teach the player the game through play rather than a long exposition sequence.

## 0:00–2:00 — Arrival

The player enters the settlement edge.

Teach:
- movement;
- camera;
- basic interaction.

Show ordinary life.

No supernatural combat yet.

## 2:00–5:00 — Something Is Wrong

The player notices a small abnormal event.

Examples:
- animals suddenly avoid a path;
- a traveler reports a strange sound;
- an object behaves unexpectedly;
- distant activity stops.

The player receives a simple objective:

**Find out what happened near the old road.**

## 5:00–8:00 — The Road

The player follows the road toward the river.

Introduce:
- optional exploration;
- environmental storytelling;
- first minor obstacle.

No lore dump.

## 8:00–10:00 — The Crossing

At the river, the player sees the first undeniable supernatural sign.

The environment briefly changes around the player.

The player continues toward the disturbance.

## 10:00–13:00 — The Veil

The damaged Veil is revealed.

The player learns:
- something is crossing the boundary;
- the world has changed;
- the disturbance is not normal.

## 13:00–15:00 — First Combat

The Veil-Torn appears.

Teach:
1. attack;
2. dodge/defense;
3. positioning;
4. reading the enemy.

The encounter ends with a short reveal or escape.

## Opening Principle

The player should finish the first 15 minutes thinking:

**“Something is seriously wrong. I want to know why.”**

Not:

**“I just watched a history lecture.”**
