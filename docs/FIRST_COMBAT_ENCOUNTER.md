# DOL First Combat Encounter

## Status
Game 1 — Combat prototype

## Goal

Teach the player the basic combat language without turning the first supernatural encounter into a boss fight.

## Enemy

**The Veil-Torn**

A DOL-original creature created by prolonged exposure to a damaged boundary.

It is not a direct adaptation of a real-world spirit or deity.

## Arena

A small clearing immediately around the damaged Veil.

The arena should include:
- readable ground;
- one or two obstacles;
- enough space to dodge;
- a visible route back;
- the Veil as the visual landmark.

Avoid clutter.

## Combat Sequence

### Phase 1 — Read

The creature appears and does not attack immediately.

Player learns:
- distance;
- enemy facing;
- movement.

### Phase 2 — First Attack

The Veil-Torn uses **Rush**.

Telegraph:
- short pause;
- body distortion;
- directional commitment.

Player lesson:
- dodge sideways or create distance.

### Phase 3 — Basic Exchange

The player can:
- light attack;
- dodge;
- reposition.

Enemy uses:
- Rush;
- Swipe.

The player should learn that attacking blindly is inefficient.

### Phase 4 — Veil Pulse

At reduced health, the creature briefly interacts with the damaged Veil.

Effect:
- short-range environmental disturbance;
- readable warning;
- player must move.

No complicated status effects.

### Phase 5 — Resolution

The creature is defeated or the player reaches the safe boundary and escapes.

Both outcomes can advance the prototype.

## Combat Principles

### Readability

Every dangerous attack needs:
- anticipation;
- action;
- recovery.

### Responsiveness

Input should feel immediate.

### Commitment

Attacks should have enough commitment to make timing meaningful, without making the first encounter frustrating.

### Recovery

The player should understand why they were hit.

Avoid invisible damage or unclear hitboxes.

## Reward

Do not drop a random treasure pile.

The first encounter rewards:
- progression;
- a new understanding of the Veil;
- access to the next story beat.

A physical item is optional and must have a clear fictional purpose.

## Failure

On defeat:
- restart near the encounter;
- preserve only what the prototype requires;
- no long replay of dialogue.

## First-Encounter Metrics

Test:
- time to first hit;
- number of successful dodges;
- damage taken;
- deaths;
- whether players understand the Rush telegraph;
- whether players recognize the Veil Pulse;
- whether the player voluntarily attacks or retreats.

## Hard Limit

The first encounter should prove:

**movement + attack + defense + enemy telegraph + environmental danger**

It should not attempt to prove the entire combat system.
